**SOLMAR CITY** — an original open-world crime game in the spirit of GTA 6. Explore the sun-soaked coastal city of Solmar on foot and by car: steal cars, outrun the police as your wanted level climbs, and work through a five-mission story for Marta Sol's crew — pickups, getaways, deliveries, escapes and one last heist at the docks. Traffic, pedestrians, combat, a HUD with a live minimap, side jobs, cash and a save.

Everything is original: the city, the characters, the vehicles, the story, the art and the sounds. Nothing is from any commercial game.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
```

Then open http://localhost:8080 in a desktop browser. No build step, no network: the game is plain static files under `game/`.

**Controls:** WASD / arrows to move, mouse to aim, click to shoot, E to enter or exit a car, Shift to sprint, P or Esc to pause, M to mute. On foot, walk up to a car and press E to steal it; while driving, W accelerates, S brakes, A/D steer.
